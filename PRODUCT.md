# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients in Cairo, Egypt, and beyond who need a credible web-development partner for restaurant, ecommerce, and local-business websites. They visit on desktop and mobile to assess Khalil AbdalMageed's craft, relevant work, technical range, and a clear route to contact. English- and Arabic-speaking visitors must receive equivalent clarity, typography, and navigation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio demonstrates web-development expertise through selected projects, case studies, research, offers, demos, and long-form writing. Its purpose is to turn a visitor's initial interest into confidence that Khalil can build a considered, reliable website for their business, then make contact easy.

## Brand Personality

Confident, editorial, restrained. The experience should feel materially considered and technically capable, with enough character to be memorable without competing with the work itself. It should communicate premium craft, calm judgment, and directness.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated interface signals: decorative gradient text, purple or cyan AI palettes, thick colored side-stripe borders, bouncy or elastic motion, image zoom hover effects, washed-out gray text on saturated backgrounds, default glassmorphism, and template-like card grids. Do not make the portfolio feel loud, gimmicky, or SaaS-generic.

## Design Principles

- Let the work establish credibility: projects, case studies, and writing must remain more prominent than decorative treatment.
- Make premium craft feel calm: hierarchy, typography, and spacing should communicate confidence without spectacle.
- Preserve a coherent bilingual experience: English and Arabic surfaces deserve equivalent readability, hierarchy, and direction-aware details.
- Favor purposeful interaction: motion and hover states should clarify affordance or orientation, never distract.
- Carry the existing gold-and-onyx identity consistently across the portfolio, content, and correspondence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve reduced-motion support for all decorative motion, maintain legible hierarchy at responsive widths, and keep LTR and RTL experiences equivalent for English and Arabic visitors.
