# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: founders, agencies, and product teams who need someone to ship polished AI app interfaces, product demos, and SaaS features with Next.js, TypeScript, and AI APIs. Secondary: restaurants, ecommerce stores, and local businesses in Cairo, Egypt, and beyond who need a credible website partner. Both groups visit on desktop and mobile, usually after hours and while comparing several candidates, to assess Khalil AbdalMageed's craft, relevant shipped work, technical range, and a clear route to contact. English- and Arabic-speaking visitors must receive equivalent clarity, typography, and navigation. AI/SaaS work leads; local-business work stays visible as a second route.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio demonstrates web-development expertise through selected projects, case studies, research, offers, demos, and long-form writing. Its purpose is to turn a visitor's initial interest into confidence that Khalil can build a considered, reliable website for their business, then make contact easy.

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, product-minded. The experience should feel like it was made by someone who ships interfaces for a living: flat, typographic, fast, and unambiguous, with enough character to be memorable without competing with the work itself. It should communicate craft, calm judgment, and directness.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual world: the Metro typographic-tile language (flat colour tiles on a solid ground, giant light-weight lowercase headlines cropped off the trailing edge, type as the interface, one accent owning every live state). Chosen 2026-09-05 to replace the earlier gold-and-onyx identity, which is now an anti-reference.
- Bilingual parity: the Arabic surface is native to the same world, not a translated Latin layout.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated interface signals: decorative gradient text, glow blobs and ambient blurred halos, gradient buttons, thick colored side-stripe borders, bouncy or elastic motion, image zoom hover effects, washed-out gray text on saturated backgrounds, default glassmorphism, and same-size icon-heading-text card grids. Avoid the discarded look: cream ground, serif display type, gold accents. Do not make the portfolio feel gimmicky or SaaS-generic.

## Design Principles

- Let the work establish credibility: projects, case studies, and writing must remain more prominent than decorative treatment.
- Make premium craft feel calm: hierarchy, typography, and spacing should communicate confidence without spectacle.
- Preserve a coherent bilingual experience: English and Arabic surfaces deserve equivalent readability, hierarchy, and direction-aware details.
- Favor purposeful interaction: motion and hover states should clarify affordance or orientation, never distract.
- Carry the Metro tile identity consistently across the portfolio, content, and correspondence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve reduced-motion support for all decorative motion, maintain legible hierarchy at responsive widths, and keep LTR and RTL experiences equivalent for English and Arabic visitors.
