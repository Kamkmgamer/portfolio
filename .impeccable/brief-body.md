# Surface brief — Home (portfolio landing)

**Scope:** the bilingual home route `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx` plus its shared chrome (Navbar). **Visitor mode:** Experience — the work leads from the first viewport.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The portfolio is a particle-detector event display: one frozen collision — the practice — with real work radiating outward as curved tracks through hairline detector rings. It refuses the template hero (name, gradient, three icon cards) and its opposite (cream serif editorial): the interface is a scientific instrument, and the instrument is the index of the work.

**OWN-WORLD:** Vacuum blue-black ground, hairline steel segmented rings, thin curved tracks in signal yellow (work) and cyan (flagship AI projects), red reserved for energy readouts, instrument panels with dim-label/bright-value rows, IBM Plex mono data + Archivo black caps display; light mode is the physics-paper print rendition. One accent, one meaning, always.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the collision resolve: tracks trace in from the vertex, each labeled with a real project; isolating a track (hover, focus, tap) dims the field and reads out its data card. Subsystem rows say who he helps; the run log carries the journey with tabular timestamps; a transmission panel carries the testimonial; dataset panels close on case studies and writing.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Left column: name in black caps, role, two-line offer, primary yellow action "See What I've Built" + ghost "Let's Talk", live status, real-count stat strip. Right ~45%: the SVG event display — four segmented rings, vertex, six focusable project tracks (two straight cyan muons = AI flagships), red calorimeter wedges, legend column with live counts. Signature interaction: track isolation.

**FORM:** Dealt catalog challenger `scientific-notation-particle-detector-event-display`, verdicted competitive, chosen by the user over the assigned Token Grammar (seed key 99e6b557). Raised: "One continuous section" from Daylight Section per the user's steer — the journey log is a single ruled strip with tabular timestamps; plus "Palette law" from CRT Arcade — one accent per meaning, effects as togglable layers.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
